# Logo Design Requirements — B2B Uzbek Export Marketplace

## Project Overview

A modern B2B digital marketplace that connects international buyers directly with verified manufacturers and producers in Uzbekistan. The platform covers key export sectors: textiles, agriculture, and minerals. The core value proposition is *trust, directness, and origin* — "straight from the source."

---

## Core Concept to Convey

The logo should feel like a bridge — connecting two worlds (global buyers ↔ Uzbek producers). It should evoke the ancient Silk Road spirit modernized for the digital era: Uzbekistan has always been a crossroads of trade, and this platform continues that legacy in a contemporary B2B context.

---

## Style

- Modern and minimal — clean geometric forms, no clutter
- Scalable and versatile (works at small sizes, on dark and light backgrounds)
- Timeless, not trendy — should feel credible to international business audiences
- No ornate traditional patterns, but subtle nods to Uzbek identity are welcome (e.g., a geometric motif inspired by Uzbek tilework or the blue of Samarkand)

---

## Symbolism to Consider

- A connecting line, arc, or link — representing direct buyer-manufacturer connection
- A subtle geographic element — a dot or origin mark for Uzbekistan
- A checkmark or shield element (optional) — suggesting verified, trusted suppliers
- Geometric forms inspired by Uzbek Islamic architecture (hexagons, stars) used in a minimal, modern way

---

## Color Direction

Suggest a palette that communicates trust, heritage, and global commerce. Potential directions:

- **Deep blue + gold** — trust + premium, echoing Uzbek mosaic colors
- **Teal + warm earth tone** — modern + grounded in origin
- **Navy + turquoise** — global professionalism + Uzbek identity

Avoid overly bright, consumer-app palettes. This is a B2B platform — the tone should be confident and professional.

---

## Typography (if included)

- Clean sans-serif, slightly geometric
- If wordmark is included, it should feel international and legible at small sizes
- Optionally pair with a short tagline like *"Direct from Uzbekistan"* or *"Verified. Direct. Uzbek."*

---

## What to Generate

Please generate several logo concepts for a B2B export marketplace called **"Made in Uzbekistan"** that visually communicates: global trade connection, Uzbekistan origin, verified suppliers, and modern minimalism. Include standalone icon/mark versions and a version with wordmark. Use the color and style directions above.
